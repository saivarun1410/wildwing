HOW TO PLAY

Controls

Press F or W to take off. You control where the bird goes and how it flies.

W / SIncrease / decrease cruising speedA / DTurn left / right↑ / ↓Climb / diveSHIFTMaximum speedSPACEFlap for liftDRAGSteerPLand on a branchH / JHunt in water / on landNBuild the nest in five tripsTSwitch between day and nightMOpen / close the large world mapQQuit to the start screen; progress is saved

On mobile, use the bottom bar: Fly, Move, Actions, and World. Move holds steering, altitude, speed, flap, and boost controls. World contains bird and weather choices, the map, day/night, sound, help, and quit.

Normal flight climbs to cloud height. Hold ↓ to dive, or ↑ to climb higher. The city center is about 800 m west-southwest of home; click the mini-map to see the full route. Fly north for snowy mountains or east for the ocean and great waterfall.
